# Wecasa

> Wecasa is a French on-demand home-services marketplace that connects households with vetted
> independent professionals who deliver the service at the customer's home. Customers book,
> schedule and pay through the Wecasa web site or the iOS/Android app; the professionals who
> fulfil the work run their business through the separate Wecasa Pro app.

Important for agents: **Wecasa publishes no public API.** There is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card and no
webhook surface. Every plausible developer hostname (api., developers., developer., docs.,
app., admin., status., partners.wecasa.fr) fails to resolve in DNS. Booking a Wecasa service
programmatically is not possible; the only routes are the consumer web app and the mobile apps.
Wecasa is a consumer of third-party APIs (Zoho, Intercom, Cloudinary, Amplitude, Prismic,
Octopush, Woosmap), not a producer of one.

## Company

- [Wecasa](https://www.wecasa.fr/): French home-services marketplace. Household cleaning and
  ironing, childcare and babysitting, mobile hairdressing, at-home beauty and nail care, massage
  and wellness, and personal fitness coaching, delivered at the customer's home.

- [Careers](https://careers.wecasa.fr/): hiring, Paris-based engineering team.

## Markets

Wecasa operates in France (wecasa.fr), the United Kingdom (wecasa.co.uk), Germany (wecasa.de),
Austria (wecasa.at) and Switzerland (wecasa.ch). Backed by Serena.

## For professionals

- [Become a Wecasa partner](https://www.wecasa.fr/pro): onboarding funnel for independent
  service professionals. This is a human recruitment form, not a technical integration — an
  applicant fills in a service-specific form, the Wecasa team calls them back, and the
  partnership is then operated entirely through the Wecasa Pro mobile app. There is no partner
  API, no sandbox and no machine-readable partner contract.
- [How the partnership works](https://help.wecasa.fr/fr/articles/3618514-le-fonctionnement-du-partenariat-avec-wecasa)

## Open source
